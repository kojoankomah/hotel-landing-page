Our story

A quieter way to experience the coast.

Cape Breeze is a fictional boutique retreat created around one simple idea: guests should feel connected to Cape Coast without giving up calm, comfort, or thoughtful service.

Natural textures, coastal colours, and locally inspired details shape every room. From here, the region's history, rainforest, beaches, and everyday energy are all within easy reach.
